Our Five Green Technologies

Sustainability is at the forefront of product development. We offer five environmentally friendly technologies to decrease carbon emissions and waste production.

XpreSole®
Made from recycled coffee grounds. Vegan, dirt-proof, water-repellent, and odor-controlled.
GreenPlax®
Shoes made from recycled plastic bottles pulled from the ocean, conserving our environment.
SiliComfy®
Superior comfort and flexibility with eco-conscious silicone-based materials.
CCILUCELL®
An ultra-lightweight, eco-friendly material offering advanced impact resistance, durability, and antimicrobial protection.
Skive-On®
An engineered outsole with 100+ independent cubes, maximizing flexibility and shock absorption.
